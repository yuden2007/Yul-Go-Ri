ABOUT US

The true treasure of Bhutan isn't found in souvenirs, but in shared smiles.

Travel Bhutan slowly, and you'll carry its peace forever.

OUR PURPOSE

Discover the Heart of Bhutan

Explore Bhutan's beauty, celebrate our culture, and support our local communities.

MISSION

  • To promote local tourism in Bhutan.
  • To share Bhutan's beautiful nature, culture, and traditions.
  • To support local businesses and communities.
  • To protect the environment and preserve our heritage.
  • To give visitors a wonderful and authentic experience.

VISION

  • To make Bhutan a popular tourism destination by sharing its beautiful nature, culture, and traditions.
  • To promote Bhutan as a beautiful place where visitors can enjoy nature, culture, and local experiences.
  • To develop local tourism in Bhutan while protecting nature and preserving our traditions.
  • To make Bhutan a better tourism destination by supporting local people and protecting our culture and environment.
